Reading the numbers

  • "Hateful" in headline counts means the model's yes (clearly hateful). borderline is reported separately and is not counted as hate.
  • Plays are not people. TikTok counts replays. "Plays beyond the followers" is reach beyond the following, not a head count.
  • No one hand-coded a sample. Every code is the model's first pass.
  • The corpus is what TikTok surfaced, not everything posted: its hashtag pages and search favour videos that already did well.

What is not here, and why

The row-level files (one row per video, post, comment, creator or request) are not published. They are personal data, not anonymous data: each video or post ID leads back to an account, captions and transcripts are their authors' words, and commenters are private people, some of them minors. TikTok's and X's terms also restrict redistributing their content. Signal4 Analyses shared these files with Eticas Foundation for this study only. Researchers who need row-level data for a specific question can contact Eticas Foundation.

About the codes

Every code is a language model's first pass; no person hand-coded the data. Do not attribute hate to a named or identifiable account on the strength of these codes alone.
